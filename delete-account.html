🗑️ Delete Your Account and Data — Score Smart

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Signing in to Score Smart is optional. If you signed in with Google, you can delete your account and your online backup at any time.

Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Score Smart and connect to the internet.
  2. Open the Tracker tab at the bottom and scroll to 👤 Account & Backup.
  3. Tap Delete my account and backup and confirm.

This deletes your sign-in and the backup saved online.

Delete by email

If you cannot open the app, email rpriya9606@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Score Smart account". We will delete your account and backup.

What is deleted

Your Google sign-in record for Score Smart (name, email and user ID) and the backup of your MCQ scores, study sessions, daily goal, subjects, completed chapters and saved questions.

What stays

The progress saved on your own phone stays there. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.
