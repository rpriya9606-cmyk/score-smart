🔒 Privacy Policy — Score Smart

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

Score Smart ("the App") is developed and maintained by an individual developer (R Priya) as a free educational tool for Karnataka 2nd PUC English students.

Information We Collect

Score Smart does not require account creation or login. The app stores your progress data (study streaks, session history, quiz scores) locally on your own device using browser local storage. This data is not transmitted to or stored on any external server, and is not accessible to the developer.

Advertising

Score Smart is currently free with no ads. In the future, the app may display advertisements (through services such as Google AdMob or AdSense) to help cover hosting and maintenance costs. If ads are introduced, this policy will be updated, and any data collected by ad providers will follow their own respective privacy policies (for example, Google's Privacy Policy).

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services (such as Google Play services) that collect information used to identify you. Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Score Smart is designed for students generally aged 16-18 (PUC level) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
