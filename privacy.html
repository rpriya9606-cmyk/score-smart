🔒 Privacy Policy — Score Smart

Effective Date: August 29, 2026  •  Last updated: October 20, 2026

Score Smart ("the App") is developed and maintained by an individual developer (R Priya) as a free educational tool for Karnataka 2nd PUC English students.

Information We Collect

Score Smart does not require account creation or login, and it does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Data Saved on Your Device

To make the app work, the following is saved only on your own phone, in the app's local storage:

  • Your MCQ scores and the questions you answered wrongly
  • Study timer sessions, daily goal, subjects you add, and badges you earn
  • Chapters you mark as completed
  • Questions you save (bookmarks)
  • Your settings, such as dark mode and the volume of focus sounds

This data is not sent to any server and is not accessible to the developer. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, this saved data is deleted.

Links to Other Websites

Some previous-year and model question papers open from Google Drive links. When you open such a link, Google's own privacy policy applies. The papers are official question papers, shown with their source for study purposes.

Advertising

Score Smart is currently free with no ads. In the future, the app may display advertisements (through services such as Google AdMob or AdSense) to help cover hosting and maintenance costs. If ads are introduced, this policy will be updated, and any data collected by ad providers will follow their own respective privacy policies (for example, Google's Privacy Policy).

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services (such as Google Play services) that collect information used to identify you. Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Score Smart is designed for students generally aged 16-18 (PUC level) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
