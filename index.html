❓ Romeo and Juliet MCQ

Score: 0 / 25

Romeo and Juliet

William Shakespeare

📌 Important Questions
1. Mention any two images to which Romeo compares Juliet.
Romeo compares Juliet to "a rich jewel in an Ethiope's ear" and "a snowy dove among crows".
2. Mention any two expressions that Juliet uses to glorify Romeo's charm.
Juliet uses expressions like "day in night" and "whiter than snow on a raven's back" to glorify Romeo.
3. Who are compared to 'a snowy dove' and 'crows' respectively?
Juliet is compared to a snowy dove, while the other people in the hall are compared to crows.
4. Mention any two qualities of night according to Juliet.
Juliet describes night as gentle and loving because it helps her meet Romeo. She also calls it black-browed night as they meet secretly.
5. What does all the world start doing and stop doing, according to Juliet, if Romeo becomes a star?
If Romeo becomes a star, people will love the night more and stop admiring the bright sun.
📘 Extra Practice
1. Mention any two similes Romeo uses to describe Juliet's beauty.
Romeo compares Juliet to "a rich jewel in an Ethiope's ear" and "a snowy dove among crows".
2. What does Romeo intend to do after the measure is done?
Romeo plans to watch Juliet's place and touch her hand to feel blessed.
3. Whose hand is referred to as 'rude'? When would it be blessed?
Romeo calls his own hand "rude". It will be blessed when he touches Juliet's hand after the dance.
4. Who would all the world be in love with at night, according to Juliet? Why?
According to Juliet, the world would love the night, because Romeo as a star would beautify the sky.
5. When and how does Juliet expect Romeo to come?
Juliet expects Romeo to come at night, like a bright star in the sky.
6. What would people forget to worship, according to Juliet? Why?
People would forget to worship the sun, because Romeo would make the night sky more beautiful.
📖 Summary (3/4 Marks)
Romeo and Juliet is one of Shakespeare's most romantic tragedies. The given extracts express the feelings of Romeo and Juliet when they see each other for the first time.

When Romeo sees Juliet on the dance floor, he is fascinated by her beauty. He feels that her beauty makes the torches burn bright and compares her to a rich jewel in an Ethiope's ear. He also compares her to a snowy dove among crows.

He decides to wait till the dance ends and then go to her and touch her hand, believing it will bless his rude hand. He realizes that he has never seen anyone as beautiful as Juliet before that night.

On the other hand, Juliet is also attracted towards Romeo and describes his charm. She calls him "day in night" and compares him to new snow on a raven's back. She asks the night to take Romeo to the sky and make him into little stars so that he will make the sky beautiful and charming. She feels that the whole world will fall in love with the night and stop worshipping the bright sun.
